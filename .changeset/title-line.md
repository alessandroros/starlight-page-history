A line under every page title shows when the page last changed, how many versions it has, and a **Page history** link, so readers find the history without scrolling to the footer. The footer link stays. The new `links` option chooses where the links go: `['title', 'footer']` (default), one of them, or `[]`. If your site overrides `PageTitle`, add `HistoryTitleLine` to your component.
